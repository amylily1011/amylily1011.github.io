AI Security

What is this?

This is my personal learning journal on AI security, topics, techniques, and concepts I’m working through, primarily via Hack The Box (HTB) and hands-on experimentation.

AI systems introduce a new class of vulnerabilities that don’t exist in traditional software. As language models get embedded into products and pipelines, understanding how they can be attacked and how to defend them is becoming an essential skill.

This section documents what I learn along the way: the attacks, the mechanics behind them, and the defenses worth knowing.
